Interview Prep Checklist — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Interview Prep Checklist helps you prepare for job interviews, with checklists, questions to ask, follow-up tracking, and offer comparison. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect: none

Interview Prep Checklist does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Interview entries, checklists, questions and answers, pitches, stories, travel plans, debrief notes, and offer details are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no internet connection.

Exports

If you export an interview brief or your full list, the export is shared through your device's system share sheet, to a destination you choose. The app does not upload exports anywhere on its own.

No ads, no analytics

The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking software. Nothing about how you use the app leaves your device.

Purchases

Optional Pro features are purchased through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we do not receive, store, or see your payment information.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+). It does not knowingly collect information from children, because it collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
