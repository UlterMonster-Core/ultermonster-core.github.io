Family Appointment Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Family Appointment Tracker keeps doctor, dentist, and school appointments for the whole family in one list, with reminders before each one. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect: none

Family Appointment Tracker does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Appointments, family-member names, locations, notes, prep checklists, and driver information are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no internet connection.

Backups

If you export a backup, it is saved as a file you pick yourself. The app does not upload backups anywhere.

No ads, no analytics

The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking software. Nothing about how you use the app leaves your device.

Purchases

Optional Pro features are purchased through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we do not receive, store, or see your payment information.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) to manage their own household appointments. Any names you enter, including those of family members, are stored only on your device and are never collected or shared by us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.

Contact
