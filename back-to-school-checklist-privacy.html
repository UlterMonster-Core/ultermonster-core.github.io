Back-to-School Checklist — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Back-to-School Checklist keeps back-to-school supply lists per kid, per grade, with quantities, prices, budgets, and first-day countdowns. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect: none

Back-to-School Checklist does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Kids' profiles, grades, schools, supply lists, quantities, prices, and notes are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no internet connection.

Sharing

If you share a combined shopping list, it is shared as plain text through your device's system share sheet, to a destination you choose. The app does not upload shared lists anywhere on its own.

No ads, no analytics

The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking software. Nothing about how you use the app leaves your device.

Purchases

Optional Pro features are purchased through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we do not receive, store, or see your payment information.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) to manage their own household shopping lists. Any names or school details you enter, including those of your children, are stored only on your device and are never collected or shared by us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
