Family Finance Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026
👨‍👩‍👧 Built for families — your data stays yours

Family Finance Tracker ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information we collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide within the app, including:

  • Transaction titles, amounts, types (income / expense / transfer), and categories.
  • Recurring bill and savings goal details.
  • Account notes and balances.

This data is stored locally on your device and securely synchronized to our cloud database exclusively using your private Family Sync Code, so every family member with the code sees the same up-to-date information.

2. How we use your information

Your information is used solely to provide, maintain, and improve the app's budgeting, expense tracking, and family synchronization features. We do not use your data for advertising, nor do we sell or rent your data to any third parties.

3. Data sharing

Data is never sold. It is synchronized to our secure cloud so that family members who possess your private Family Sync Code can view and edit the shared family data — that is how Family Sync works. We do not share your data with advertisers or any unrelated third parties. Anyone with your code can view and edit the shared family data, so keep your code private.

4. Data deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • In the app: transactions, recurring bills and income, category budgets, and savings goals can each be deleted from their respective screens. Deleted records are removed from your device and from Family Sync.
  • Full erasure: to request complete deletion of all data associated with your Family Sync Code, email ultermonster@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and include your Family Sync Code. We will delete all synced records for that code within 30 days and confirm by reply.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored locally on your device.

We do not keep copies of records you delete, and we do not retain your financial data after your deletion request is fulfilled.

5. Data security

We implement robust security measures to protect your financial data. Data transmitted to the cloud is encrypted in transit, and access to your family's data requires the unique Family Sync Code.

6. Notifications

Bill payment reminders are local notifications generated on your device. They contain no tracking and transmit nothing off your phone.

7. Children's privacy

Family Finance Tracker is intended for adults (18+) managing household finances and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the developer support contact on Google Play or at ultermonster@gmail.com.
