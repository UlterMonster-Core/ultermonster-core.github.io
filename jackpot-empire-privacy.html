Jackpot Empire — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026
🔒 100% on-device — we collect nothing

Jackpot Empire ("the app") is developed by UlterMonster Core. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

1. No data collection

Jackpot Empire does not collect, transmit, store on servers, sell, or share any personal data. All game state — chip balance, level and XP, achievements, settings, free-play tickets — is stored exclusively in a private database on your own device. There is no user account, no sign-up, and no cloud sync.

2. Virtual currency & purchases

Jackpot Empire is a social casino. All chips are virtual: they cannot be redeemed for money or prizes. Optional chip packs and the VIP Club subscription are offered as in-app purchases processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not see or handle your payment information; Google's own privacy policy applies to the purchase flow.

3. Third-party services

The app uses standard Android platform components only, plus Google Play Billing for optional chip packs and the VIP Club subscription. We integrate no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs. The app verifies it was installed from Google Play using the on-device package manager; this check involves no data leaving your device.

4. Age requirement

Jackpot Empire is intended for adults (18+) and contains simulated gambling. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children — because it collects no data at all.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
