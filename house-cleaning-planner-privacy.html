House Cleaning Planner — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

House Cleaning Planner is a personal household cleaning checklist for your own house. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect: none

House Cleaning Planner does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything you enter — tasks, rooms, minutes estimates, notes, streaks, and history — is stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no internet connection.

No ads, no analytics

The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking software. Nothing about how you use the app leaves your device.

Purchases

Optional Pro features are purchased through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we do not receive, store, or see your payment information.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+). It does not knowingly collect information from children, because it collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
