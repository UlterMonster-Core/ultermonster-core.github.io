My Paycheck Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

My Paycheck Tracker checks pay from a job: pay periods, expected vs. actual amounts, payday countdowns, deduction breakdowns, and totals. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect: none

My Paycheck Tracker does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Pay periods, employers, hours, amounts, deductions, and notes are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync, no internet connection, and no permissions at all.

Exports

If you export a CSV, it is saved through your device's system file picker, to a location you choose. The app does not upload exports anywhere.

No ads, no analytics

The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking software. Nothing about how you use the app leaves your device.

Purchases

Optional Pro features are purchased through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we do not receive, store, or see your payment information.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+). It does not knowingly collect information from children, because it collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
