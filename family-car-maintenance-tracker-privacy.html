Family Car Maintenance Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Family Car Maintenance Tracker keeps vehicle service records, reminders, fuel logs, spending, and document renewals in one place. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect: none

Family Car Maintenance Tracker does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Vehicles, service records, mileage, costs, fuel fill-ups, documents, and notes are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no internet connection.

Sharing

If you share a vehicle's history, it is shared as text through your device's system share sheet, to a destination you choose. The app does not upload shared histories anywhere on its own.

No ads, no analytics

The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking software. Nothing about how you use the app leaves your device.

Purchases

Optional Pro features are purchased through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we do not receive, store, or see your payment information.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+). It does not knowingly collect information from children, because it collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
