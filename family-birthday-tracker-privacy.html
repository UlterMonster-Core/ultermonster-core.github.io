Family Birthday Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Family Birthday Tracker keeps birthdays for the whole family in one list, with countdowns, reminders, gift ideas, and budgets. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect: none

Family Birthday Tracker does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Names, birthdays, photos, notes, gift ideas, gift budgets, gift history, and prep checklists are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync, and no internet connection.

Contacts import

If you use the contacts import feature, the app reads birthdays from your phone's contacts on your device only, so you can add them to your list. Imported birthdays are never transmitted anywhere.

Backups

If you export a backup, it is saved as a file you keep yourself. The app does not upload backups anywhere.

No ads, no analytics

The app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking software. Nothing about how you use the app leaves your device.

Purchases

Optional Premium features are purchased through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we do not receive, store, or see your payment information.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) to manage their own household lists. Any names or birthdays you enter, including those of family members, are stored only on your device and are never collected or shared by us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
