SHOWTRACKR: TV Guide Canada — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026
🔒 We collect nothing — your data stays on your device

App: SHOWTRACKR: TV Guide Canada (Google Play package name: com.tvshowst)
Developer: UlterMonster Core
Legal entity: This app is developed and operated by Thomas Stoehr, an independent developer based in St. Catharines, Ontario, Canada, publishing on Google Play under the developer name UlterMonster Core.

This privacy policy explains how SHOWTRACKR: TV Guide Canada ("the app") handles your information. The short version: the app does not collect any personal data.

1. No personal data collected

The app does not collect, store on servers, sell, or share any personal information. There is no user account, no sign-up, and no login. The app does not request or access:

  • Names or email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Contacts
  • Photos or files
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers

2. Local-only storage

Any data you create in the app — such as the shows you track, episodes you mark as watched, and your preferences — is stored locally on your own device only. It never leaves your device, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Show information retrieval

To display TV show information, episode dates, and where-to-watch availability in Canada, the app connects directly from your device to third-party TV data services to retrieve that content. Search terms you type are sent to those services only so they can return matching results. UlterMonster Core does not operate servers that receive or store your searches or any other usage data.

4. No tracking, analytics, or advertising

The app does not use analytics tools, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, cookies, or third-party data collection services.

5. Purchases

Optional SHOWTRACKR Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not see or handle your payment information; Google's own privacy policy applies to the purchase flow.

6. No data sharing

Because the app does not collect data, it does not share any data with third parties, advertisers, analytics providers, or external services.

7. Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy: ultermonster@gmail.com
